# Founder vision (captured from the founder's own words, October 2026)

Who: a solo founder / dev studio who needs the business to support a family. Existing production stack: CatholicJoe (Astro 7 on Cloudflare Workers, D1, better-auth, Stripe Checkout, ShipStation, Cloudflare Email, Mailchimp).

Operating model
- Remote-first. The product, onboarding, setup and support must work for a business anywhere without a visit.
- Start slow and personal with businesses nearby to build the brand and the first relationships; the founder can drive to a restaurant or shop to set it up in person as a brand tactic, never as something the business depends on.
- The product must eventually scale without the founder as the constraint.

Customers
- Any small local business, any size of need: a restaurant, a local shop or retailer, the founder's family auto parts store with its full inventory online, or a business that just needs a modern presence site "that looks like it is from this decade".
- Primary targets: businesses with an address and a phone number but no website. A reliable way to find them is required.
- Secondary targets: businesses that already have a website (often on an older or more expensive platform) and need integrations to switch.
- Price range: from a $500 website to $40k-$100k custom-built software (for example inventory management for a retailer).

Product
- One reusable engine; never rebuild the engine for each client. Only the frontend changes the most.
- Variants on the engine: food (integrates with DoorDash and other food platforms), clothing/retail (Shopify-style store with all products and lots of photos), presence/services, custom.
- A dashboard for the business owner with an app-store-like menu of integrations (Mailchimp, ShipStation, Stripe, PayPal, etc.), using OAuth connectors wherever possible so owners never handle API keys, with API keys and webhooks as the fallback.
- A founder dashboard to find, track and manage customers and link each to its Cloudflare Worker site.
- Long term: self-serve where the owner types what they want and an AI builds and adjusts the site from the engine's blocks by conversation.

Questions the founder wants answered
1. Which business types to focus on first, ranked, with a way to find those without websites.
2. Which integrations secondary customers need, and how to offer them as an app menu with OAuth.
3. Does this business make sense?
4. Can a solo founder make it succeed?
5. Who is the competition?
